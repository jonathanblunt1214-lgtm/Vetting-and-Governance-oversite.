# Independent Oversight — Nexus / Crucible Governing Plan

This repository is the independent governance and oversight authority for the registered Crucible organism. It is not product code and does not assimilate inspected content.

## Oversight scope

The registered organism includes The Crucible, Learning-Worker, Crucible-Vetted-Learning-State, and other explicitly registered Crucible components. Nexus, NVIDIA-NIM-CONSOLE, and AI Collaboration remain integration peers/consumers unless explicitly registered in the oversight inventory.

## Authority

Oversight independently vets bounded manifests, audits registered components, verifies custody/provenance, can issue a signed fail-closed STOP, controls acceptance of candidate-only worker exports, and independently signs oversight results.

Oversight cannot become a learning system from inspected data, rewrite Crucible runtime state, grant scientific proof, replace owner authorization, or silently clear a STOP.

## Learning custody relationship

Learning-Worker → candidate-only export → independent oversight → signed approval/encrypted delivery → Crucible-Vetted-Learning-State → The-Crucible scientific-learning pipeline.

Oversight approval establishes custody eligibility only. It never establishes the truth of a claim.

## Nexus relationship

Nexus hosts the integrated coding architecture, but hosting does not weaken independent oversight. The console and AI Collaboration layer must treat Crucible verification and oversight states as authoritative boundaries rather than bypasses.

## Plan

1. Keep the component inventory explicit and oversight-owned.
2. Verify each registered component's repository, ref, role, and custody boundary.
3. Independently inspect worker exports against exact lineage and source hashes.
4. Reject stale, altered, unapproved, or promoted material at the oversight boundary.
5. Maintain signed STOP/CLEAR semantics and fail-closed restoration.
6. Verify the complete learning custody path.
7. Verify the Nexus coding path without granting Nexus or AI Collaboration authority over oversight.
8. Record exact evidence, timestamps, hashes, and unresolved findings for every governed integration change.
